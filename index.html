About Me

Who I Am

I came to data science through medicine and finance — not the typical route. I studied general medicine in Afghanistan, then finance at the American University of Afghanistan, where I discovered data science and knew immediately that was where I was headed.

That background is not a detour. It gives me domain fluency most DS candidates lack — I understand clinical data, health outcomes, and the real stakes of model decisions in humanitarian and healthcare contexts.

When the Taliban took over, I continued my education in Qatar with the support of AUAF, then earned a second full scholarship to the University of New Haven. I am building toward a December 2026 M.S. and looking for internship or junior roles where I can contribute and keep growing fast.
